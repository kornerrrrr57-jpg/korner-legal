Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Korner is a GPS lap timer and telemetry recorder for motorsport. This policy explains what the app uses, where that information goes, and what you can do about it. It covers the Korner iPhone app.

1. The short version

  • No accounts. Korner does not ask for your name, email or a password.
  • Your racing data stays on your phone. Sessions, laps, telemetry, tracks and records are stored only in the app's local database. We do not run a server that receives them, and we cannot see them.
  • Korner uses precise location while you run a session, because that is how a lap timer works.
  • No ads, no advertising trackers, and we never sell data.
  • The app is free. You can leave an optional tip through Apple; it unlocks nothing, and we never see your payment details.
  • The only things that leave your phone are crash reports, problem reports you choose to send, weather lookups, and the map shown when you create or edit a track (with the data Google's map software sends), described below.

2. What the app uses on your phone

Location

Korner records precise GPS position, speed and heading while a session is running, including while the app is in the background so timing continues when the screen is off or your phone is in your pocket. It also reads your location when the Drive tab is open, to show local weather, and in Pre-race setup, to list your saved tracks nearest first. Location is not used at any other time.

Motion and connected devices

Accelerometer readings are used to calculate G-force. If you connect a Bluetooth GPS receiver or an OBD-II adapter, Korner also records what those devices report, such as higher-rate position data, engine RPM and throttle position.

Photos

Korner only writes to your photo library, when you choose to save a lap-time poster. It does not read your photos.

All of the above is stored only on your device.

4. How long it is kept

Data on your phone stays until you erase it in the app or delete the app. Crash diagnostics and problem reports are kept by Sentry for a limited period and then deleted automatically. If you gave your email so we could reply, we use it only for that conversation.

5. Your choices

  • Erase everything: Profile › Settings › Erase local data. This cannot be undone, since there is no cloud copy. Deleting the app also deletes its data.
  • Export your data: open a session, tap the ⋯ button and choose Export CSV.
  • Turn off location at any time in iOS Settings. Lap timing will not work without it.

Because we do not hold your racing data, there is nothing on our side to access or delete. For questions about crash or problem reports, or to have a report you sent deleted, contact us.

6. Children

Korner is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

7. Security

Your data is protected by your phone's own security. Anything the app sends, such as crash reports and weather requests, is encrypted in transit with TLS.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top. If a future version adds features that send your data anywhere new, such as optional cloud backup, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
