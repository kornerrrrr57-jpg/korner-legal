Common questions

Does Korner cost anything?

No. Korner is free, with no ads and no subscriptions. If you'd like to support it, you can leave an optional tip under Settings › Buy me a coffee. Tips don't unlock anything.

Do I need an account?

No. Korner has no accounts. Everything you record is stored on your iPhone.

My laps aren't being timed.

  • Make sure location access is set to While Using the App or Always in iOS Settings › Korner › Location. Always is needed for Pocket mode, where timing continues with the screen off.
  • Check that your track's start/finish line spans the full width of the track. If it doesn't, create the track again and place the line across the whole track.
  • Phone GPS updates about once a second. For tighter timing, pair a 10 Hz+ Bluetooth receiver such as a RaceBox under Bluetooth on the Drive tab.

How do I back up my sessions?

Open a session, tap the ⋯ button and choose Export CSV. You can import Korner CSV files again from Import a session on the Drive tab.

How do I delete my data?

Profile › Settings › Erase local data, or delete the app. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

Is it safe to use on the road?

No. Use Korner only on closed circuits and private tracks, and never interact with your phone while driving.
